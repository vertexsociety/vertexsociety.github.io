We are gathered in pursuit of knowledge and applying the gift that we have

The faculty of thought is the strongest prerogative of humans. The finest applications of this faculty are uncovering and categorizing, thus making order out of chaos. This summarizes the essence of the human capacity to understand (lat. intelligere), around which participation in Vertex Society is formed.
