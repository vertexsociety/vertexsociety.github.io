Fellows

All Vertex Society Fellows enjoy same rights and privileges within the society. Fellows are listed alongside their first spoken languages (not exclusively equal to mother tongue) and last reported places of residence.

Distinguished Research Fellows

A Fellow with both a Ph.D. and a Distinguished Fellow title will be known as Distinguished Research Fellow.

Research Fellows

As sign of recognition of personal achievements and contributions to the general body of knowledge, Vertex Society confers the title of Research Fellow to all Fellows with the earned Ph.D.

Fellows earning their Ph.D. past the date of becoming Vertex Society Fellows need to notify the society upon receiving their Ph.D. so that they can be promoted to Research Fellows.
