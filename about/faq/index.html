FAQ

    1. Why another society?
FSEPG Vertex is aimed at filling a large gap that previously existed among societies between 3 and 4 standard deviations, and being one of the very few that uses professional, standardized, supervised tests only selecting at the professed level of intelligence beyond any doubt. FSEPG Vertex will remain dedicated to the use of professional, standardized tests only, along with only a small number of societies out there.
    1. Who verifies the qualifications of the founder?
Unlike some known cases of founders below cut-offs of their societies, the founder of FSEPG Vertex stands ready to openly supply his credentials as per the list of qualifying scores on professional, standardized tests and indeed, should such concerns arise, will place relevant documents accessible on the site.
